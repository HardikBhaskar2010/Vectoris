# Vectoris Engineering Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

As contributors and maintainers of the **Vectoris Workstation**, we pledge to foster an inclusive, collaborative, and highly professional engineering culture focused on craft, security, and technical excellence.

---

## Standards of Technical Craft

We commit to:
- Writing honest, robust, and mathematically sound estimating software.
- Treating user data and CAD blueprints as strictly confidential and local-first.
- Prioritizing security, stability, and failure recovery over quick visual hacks.
- Reviewing code constructively, offering actionable feedback, and maintaining high code standards.
- Practicing transparent engineering communication without fabricated metrics or telemetry.

---

## Enforcement & Leadership

The Vectoris Engineering Leadership team is responsible for clarifying and upholding these standards.

For inquiries or code standard discussions, reach out to the core engineering maintainers at `engineering@vectoris.ai`.
