## 1. Intent

Vectoris must feel: premium, calm, intelligent, engineering-grade, spatial, minimal, trustworthy, sophisticated. It must explicitly **not** look like "a generic AI dashboard with purple gradients."

Design direction, per founder: premium Apple-like feel, spacious, minimalistic, liquid glass, refined, high-quality motion, full system-theme support (dark + light).

## 2. Why This Matters for This Product Specifically

Vectoris operates in high-stakes engineering/commercial workflows where a wrong takeoff can cost real margin. The interface must communicate precision and restraint, not novelty. Generic "AI hype" visual language (gradients, glowing orbs, chat-bubble-everywhere) undermines the trust the product needs — see `UX_PRINCIPLES.md` for how this connects to hiding confidence scores and keeping AI explanation restrained.
